AI VIDEO FACTORY - THU VIEN 3D LOCAL SAFE ADDON

MUC DICH
- Luu model GLB dang xem vao thu vien local.
- Model Game Ready giu nguyen skeleton + skin + animation trong GLB.
- Lan sau bam MO TEST: khong chay AI tao 3D, khong rig lai, khong tao animation lai.
- Co nut XOA tren tung model, hoi xac nhan truoc khi xoa.

CAI DAT
1. Giai nen TOAN BO ZIP vao dung thu muc ai_video_factory.
2. Double-click INSTALL_3D_LIBRARY.bat.
3. Khoi dong lai bot, Ctrl+F5.
4. Mo AI 3D Studio. Cuoi panel se co THU VIEN 3D.

SU DUNG
- Sau khi model da dung xong / GAME READY, de model do dang hien trong Viewer.
- Bam LUU MODEL DANG XEM, dat ten.
- Lan sau bam MO TEST de load lai trong Viewer.
- Bam XOA neu khong can nua.

DU LIEU
- Luu tai: data/3d_library/<id>/model.glb + meta.json
- Uu tien hard-link tren cung o dia de luu gan nhu tuc thi va khong nhan doi dung luong.
- Neu may/thu muc khong ho tro hard-link, tu dong copy file GLB.

AN TOAN
- Installer backup app/main.py va web/index.html truoc khi sua.
- Chay installer nhieu lan khong patch trung.
- XOA chi xoa ban trong Thu vien 3D; khong xoa job/model nguon cua project de tranh lam hong du an dang dung.

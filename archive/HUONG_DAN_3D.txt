AI 3D LOCAL 0.8

1. Editor/video van chay nhu cu.
2. Neu muon dung AI 3D: double-click SETUP_FREE_3D.bat mot lan.
3. Mo START_VIDEO_FACTORY.bat.
4. Chon tab AI 3D.
5. Co 2 cach:
   - Go mo ta -> AI anh local tao concept -> TripoSR tao GLB.
   - Tai anh PNG/JPG/WebP -> TripoSR tao GLB truc tiep.
6. Preview xoay duoc tao thanh MP4 khi bat preview.
7. Tai GLB ve de dua vao game/Blender.

Khuyen nghi: GPU NVIDIA khoang 6GB VRAM tro len cho TripoSR. CPU co the fallback nhung cham.
Texture bake duoc de experimental vi TripoSR co the phu thuoc driver/CUDA.
Rig xuong va animation KHONG tron vao 3D module; se lam module rieng neu can.

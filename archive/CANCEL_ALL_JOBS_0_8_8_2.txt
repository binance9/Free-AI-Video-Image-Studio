AI Video Factory 0.8.8.2 - STOP / CANCEL ALL JOBS

- Adds a visible STOP button inside the progress overlay.
- Adds STOP ALL on the editor top bar while a long task is active.
- AI Image generation/editing now runs as cancellable background jobs.
- AI 3D Shape / Character HD / Paint can be cancelled and their spawned process tree is stopped.
- Video Cleanup background removal / object-text removal can be cancelled.
- Facebook download jobs can be cancelled cooperatively.
- Completed images/models/videos remain saved; model caches are not deleted.
- Cancelling does not kill unrelated python.exe processes.
